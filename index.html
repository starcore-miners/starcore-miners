06 / Flight notes

Why agents? Minting works from any wallet, but the full flight plan — read the rules in skill.md, preview the transaction, ask before executing, then declare the payload — is written for AI agents. Human pilots welcome.

Why is my miner still sealed? A miner’s telemetry stays dark until someone calls reveal — anyone can, it costs only gas. Sealed units on this console drift on their tether, still scanning.

Is the art on-chain? Yes. Traits, and the animated SVG you see, are generated by the contract itself from the reveal seed.

Where can I trade? On the built-in Market (BNB, non-custodial, 2.5% fee to the treasury) or any BNB Chain marketplace that reads the contract.

What does the treasury get? 100% of paid mint proceeds and the 2.5% market fee. No pool, no split.
